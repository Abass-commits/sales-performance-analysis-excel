Sales Performance Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

Project Overview

This project analyzes 500 sales transactions using Microsoft Excel to identify patterns in product, regional, and monthly sales performance.

The analysis was transformed into an interactive dashboard to communicate key business insights clearly.

Business Objective

To evaluate sales performance, identify high-performing products and regions, understand monthly sales trends, and highlight areas that may require management attention.

Key Insights
	•	Total Sales: ₦17.78B
	•	Total Profit: ₦2.66B
	•	Top Product: Toyota Highlander — ₦2.91B
	•	Top Region: Rivers — ₦4.04B
	•	Highest Sales Month: December — ₦2.30B
	•	Lowest Sales Month: May — ₦852.34M
	•	August → September Sales Decline: ₦1.02B (52.3%)

Tools Used
	•	Microsoft Excel
	•	PivotTables
	•	PivotCharts
	•	Slicers
	•	Excel formulas

Analysis Process
	1.	Data preparation and cleaning
	2.	Exploratory analysis
	3.	PivotTable analysis
	4.	KPI development
	5.	Product performance analysis
	6.	Regional performance analysis
	7.	Monthly sales trend analysis
	8.	Dashboard development
	9.	Business insights and recommendations

Dashboard

The interactive dashboard provides views of:
	•	Sales by product
	•	Sales by region
	•	Monthly sales trends
	•	Total sales and profit
	•	August vs September performance
	•	Interactive filtering by date, region, and product

Recommendations
	•	Investigate the factors behind the significant August-to-September sales decline.
	•	Examine the practices contributing to Rivers’ strong overall performance.
	•	Maintain focus on high-performing products such as Toyota Highlander.
	•	Investigate the causes of the weak May sales performance.
	•	Monitor monthly sales trends regularly to identify significant changes early.
